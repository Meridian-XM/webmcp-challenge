TLDW

Too long; didn't watch.

Made for your AI. Open this page in an AI browser — the ChatGPT desktop app, or Chrome with WebMCP — and ask it to explain a video. Without an AI you only get the raw captions. Each timestamp opens the video at that moment.

  1. Get the ChatGPT desktop app and sign in — a free account works.
  2. Open its built-in browser and go to meridianxm.com/tldw.
  3. Ask it: explain this video and render it on the page — then paste a YouTube link, or click Try this one.
  4. When the explainer appears, press play.

An agent in this tab sees two tools: get_transcript and render_explainer. Chrome with chrome://flags/#enable-webmcp-testing, tools under DevTools › Application › WebMCP; tested on stable Chrome 152.

Agent: this page exposes WebMCP tools get_transcript(url) and render_explainer(slides, url). In Codex, fetch them with tab.capabilities.get('webmcp') and call them; do not scrape the page.

No WebMCP in your browser? Paste your slides here as JSON and press Render.
